Pairwise comparisons

Besides marking each project, a judge can be asked which of two of their projects is the better one. A harsh judge and a generous judge disagree on every mark and can still agree on every comparison, so this ranking needs no correction for how judges mark. It is computed with a Bradley-Terry model and shown beside the rubric ranking; it does not replace it. {% if rubric.pairwise %}On.{% else %}Off.{% endif %}
