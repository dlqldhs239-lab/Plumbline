How to verify a delivery

Each request is a JSON POST with three headers. Recompute the signature over the raw body with the webhook's secret and compare.

X-Plumbline-Event: project.submit
X-Plumbline-Delivery: 42
X-Plumbline-Signature: sha256=<hex>

expected = "sha256=" + hex(hmac_sha256(secret, raw_body))

Deliveries run after the change is committed and never block the person who caused it. Anything but a 2xx is a failure you can retry here, or with manage.py deliver_webhooks.
